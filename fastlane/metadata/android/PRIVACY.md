# Privacy Policy for Sele for Android

Effective November 16, 2026.

Sele is provided by **Chipi & Chapa Corp.** Contact:
**chipi.chapa.corporation@gmail.com**.

## What the app does

Sele for Android connects to Sele on a computer you select and approve. The phone
does not run coding-agent services itself. Your computer handles providers, project
files, Git, terminals, and conversation storage. Sele does not require a Sele cloud
account and does not provide a hosted relay for this connection.

## Data handled by Sele

The app transfers prompts, conversations, selected attachments, file contents,
terminal input, browser URL metadata, preferences, and workspace actions to your
paired computer as needed for the features you use. Your computer sends workspace
and conversation data back to your phone. Drafts and preferences can be retained on
both devices. Clearing phone storage does not erase copies stored on your computer.

The app sends your phone model, a pairing credential, and a device identifier to the
paired computer. On supported devices, this identifier is derived from Android's
identifier scoped to the app signing key, device, and Android user. It is used to
recognize and manage pairings; it is not an advertising identifier.

The Android app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Chipi & Chapa Corp.
does not operate a Sele server that automatically receives your conversations or
project files. If you send us a support email or choose to share diagnostic files,
we receive the information you include and use it to respond to your request.

## AI providers and other websites

The coding providers configured on your computer may transmit content to their
services. Their terms, privacy policies, authentication, retention settings, and
data practices apply to those requests. Review your provider configuration before
sending private content.

Pages you open in Browser, external links, remote images, and supported visualization
libraries may contact third-party websites or content delivery networks. Those
services receive the requests required to load their content and may apply their
own privacy policies. Browser cookies and site storage are held by Android WebView.

## Local network security

The Sele control connection between your Android device and paired computer uses
TLS encryption. Compare the security codes shown on both devices before approving
first pairing. Android remembers the approved computer certificate and rejects an
unexpected certificate change. Nearby discovery advertises public computer identity
and connection information; it does not advertise access credentials.

Keep desktop access on your local network. Pages you choose to open in Browser may
use HTTP or HTTPS according to their own URLs, and their security is separate from
Sele's encrypted control connection. Requests made by desktop coding providers are
also governed by those providers' terms and privacy policies.

## Permissions and files

Network and Wi-Fi multicast access support communication and nearby-computer
discovery. The app does not request contacts, advertising ID, precise location,
microphone, or broad storage permissions. Android's file picker lets you choose the
files you upload or where to save downloads. Only upload content you intend to make
available to the paired computer and its configured providers.

## Retention and deletion

Uninstalling Sele or clearing its Android app data removes phone-side pairings,
drafts, preferences, and browser data. Remove the device in desktop Sele's Local
network settings to revoke its desktop approval. Manage or delete conversations,
attachments, projects, and provider data on the computer and with the applicable
provider; uninstalling the phone app does not delete those copies.

Diagnostic exports are saved only when you request them. Review a diagnostic file
before sending it to another person. Files saved to a location you choose remain
there until you delete them.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when Sele's behavior changes. The published policy will
show its effective date. Contact Chipi & Chapa Corp. at
chipi.chapa.corporation@gmail.com with privacy or support questions.
